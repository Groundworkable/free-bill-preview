Groundwork · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Groundwork is built around sensitive parts of real life. This policy explains what Able Systems LLC collects, why we use it, and the choices you have when you use the public site or invitation-only private beta.

Version: privacy-v1Effective October 3, 2026Operator: Able Systems LLC
Plain-language summary: We collect the information you choose to give Groundwork, limited technical information needed to operate and secure it, and public-site analytics. We use that information to run the beta, improve Groundwork, communicate with you, and protect the service. Able Systems does not sell personal information.

1. Information we collect

The information depends on how you interact with Groundwork.

  • Beta-access requests: name, email address, what interests you about Groundwork, anything you choose to write in the request form, eligibility confirmations, and communication choices.
  • Private-beta account information: email address, authentication and security information, account settings, legal-acceptance records, and records needed for account recovery and support.
  • Information you enter into Groundwork: responsibilities, income, savings, Money Locations, planning information, working-time information, notes, and other records you choose to enter.
  • Support and feedback: messages, feedback, bug reports, and other information you send when contacting us or participating in the beta.
  • Technical and usage information: browser/device information, pages or features used, timestamps, security logs, and similar operational data. The public site currently uses Google Analytics.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and secure Groundwork.
  • Review private-beta access requests and send invitations when access is approved.
  • Authenticate users, provide account recovery, record required legal acceptance, and respond to support requests.
  • Calculate and display the planning views and working-life information you ask Groundwork to produce.
  • Understand beta usage, diagnose problems, and improve Groundwork.
  • Send required service, security, access, and support communications.
  • Send optional development or product updates when you choose to receive them.
  • Comply with law, enforce our terms, investigate security incidents, and protect Groundwork, Able Systems, users, and others.

3. Service providers

We use service providers to operate Groundwork and the public site. Current providers may include GitHub Pages for public-site hosting, Cloudflare for domain and application-delivery infrastructure, Supabase for database and authentication infrastructure, Resend for email delivery, Google Analytics for public-site analytics, and Google Apps Script or related Google services for processing beta-access requests. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and according to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Analytics and similar technologies

The public site currently uses Google Analytics to understand general site use. Analytics services may use cookies, device or browser information, network information, and similar identifiers to provide usage measurements. You can use browser or device controls to limit cookies where supported. Groundwork does not use this policy to authorize the sale of personal information or targeted-advertising profiles.

5. Financial and life-planning information

Groundwork is a planning and information tool, not a bank or financial institution. You control what financial and life-planning information you choose to enter. Do not place passwords, full payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other secrets into ordinary notes or fields unless Groundwork specifically asks for that information through a feature designed for it.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Able Systems does not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers as described above, when required by law or legal process, to protect rights or safety, to investigate or respond to misuse or security incidents, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of all or part of the business.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the beta, provide your account, maintain security and records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Groundwork includes account-deletion controls for invited users where available. Some backup, security, legal-acceptance, or provider records may remain for a limited period when reasonably necessary or required by law.

8. Security and security incidents

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a strong unique password, protect your account, and be thoughtful about the information you choose to enter.

If a security incident affects information for which applicable law requires notice, Able Systems will investigate and provide notices to affected individuals, regulators, or others as required by applicable law.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You may unsubscribe from optional development or product updates using the instructions in those messages. Required account, security, access, and service communications are separate from optional marketing or product updates.

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights concerning personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, or a copy of information we maintain about you. Where applicable law grants such a right, you may submit a request to hello@groundworkbyable.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it, and legal exceptions may apply.

10. Age and geographic eligibility

The Groundwork private beta is currently intended only for adults age 18 or older who reside in the United States. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly maintain private-beta accounts for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to Groundwork, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to this policy

Groundwork is in private beta, so our practices may evolve. We will update this page when this policy changes and provide additional notice when a change is material and appropriate.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@groundworkbyable.com.
