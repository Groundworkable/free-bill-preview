Why money = working life

A dollar amount is only half the cost.

Every responsibility makes a claim on two things at once: your money and the time you exchange to create it. Groundwork keeps both visible, so “Can I afford it?” becomes a better question: “What does carrying this actually require of me?”

01
Responsibilities become life overhead.

The recurring things you carry stop disappearing into a pile of transactions. Groundwork gives them a place in the operating picture.

02

03
Savings can mean time you no longer have to sell.

What Groundwork helps you manage

The pieces stop living in separate rooms.

Groundwork connects the parts of life that usually get tracked alone. Responsibilities affect income. Income costs time. Savings change what future work has to carry. Progress only makes sense when you can see the relationship.
