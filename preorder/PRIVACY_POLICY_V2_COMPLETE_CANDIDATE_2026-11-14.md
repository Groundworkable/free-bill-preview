## Privacy Policy — proposed public copy

Groundwork by Able is operated by Able Systems LLC (“Able Systems,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). Groundwork helps people understand money, time, responsibilities, and choices. This Privacy Policy explains the information we handle when you visit our public website, request or use invitation-only beta access, contact us, or—**if made available**—make a one-time pre-purchase of future Groundwork membership.

The payment pre-purchase flow is not currently open. Where this policy describes that flow, it explains how information would be handled if that flow is launched; it does not imply that paid memberships, bank connections, or future product capabilities are available now.

### 1. Information we collect

What we collect depends on the service you use:

- **Public website and early/beta access requests:** your name, email address, interest in Groundwork, eligibility and communication choices, and anything you submit in an access request or contact form.
- **Invited beta accounts:** your email address, sign-in/authentication and security details, account preferences, records of consent to terms where applicable, and recovery or support records.
- **Your Groundwork entries:** information you choose to enter, such as responsibilities, income, spending, savings, Money Locations, goals, working-time calculations, notes and other planning records. These may contain personal financial information.
- **Support and feedback:** messages, help requests, bug reports and information you voluntarily share with us by email or through available service channels.
- **Technical and analytics data:** limited browser or device attributes, interactions with the website or beta, network identifiers such as IP addresses where supplied by the relevant service, time stamps, security/diagnostic logs, and public-site analytics. The public site currently uses Google Analytics.
- **One-time pre-purchases, if offered:** the hosted payment provider collects purchase and billing details needed for payment, including contact/billing information and payment-method information. We may receive and maintain payment-related records and references, as explained below. We do not ask customers to enter full card numbers or card verification codes in ordinary Groundwork forms or email.

### 2. Payment, pre-purchase, and refund information

If we open a one-time pre-purchase for future Groundwork membership, checkout will be hosted by **Stripe**, which processes payment and related billing information under its own applicable privacy practices. Stripe—not the Groundwork website or isolated payment Worker—collects sensitive card-entry information at checkout.

Our proposed isolated payment service is designed to record limited **transaction evidence** returned through verified Stripe test/production flows: a Stripe Checkout Session ID, PaymentIntent ID, Stripe Customer ID if present, the purchased offer and price identifiers, the selected term, recorded acceptance version, paid/unfulfilled order status, currency, subtotal, tax and total, event references and timestamps. The refund/dispute journal can retain event IDs, refund/dispute IDs, payment or charge references, amounts, currency, status and receipt times. These records may be used to document a purchase, reconcile refunds/disputes, and satisfy legitimate financial and legal obligations.

**Important distinction:** The current isolated order database does **not** store the buyer's email address, Groundwork application account ID, or an access entitlement, and does not itself grant membership. The customer may provide an email or purchase reference when contacting billing support. A verified purchase or Stripe receipt alone does not establish usable Groundwork access. The actual process for matching payment records to the purchaser, delivering notices and activating legitimately purchased access must be completed and accurately explained before paid checkout opens.

Buying a one-time pre-purchase does **not** authorize recurring charges or automatically subscribe you to optional marketing. If a refund or charge dispute occurs, we may process records required to investigate or resolve it without altering the original payment evidence.

### 3. How we use information

We use information as appropriate to:

- Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website and beta service;
- Review access requests, authenticate invited users and offer account or support help;
- Produce the planning calculations and views requested by users;
- Analyze general site/beta usage, investigate bugs and prevent abuse or unauthorized activity;
- Process and verify a future pre-purchase if offered, maintain order records, address refunds, cancellations and disputes, reconcile tax and financial records, and notify customers about material service or delivery changes;
- Deliver necessary security, account, service and purchase-related communications; 
- Send optional product or development updates only where the recipient has chosen to receive them or where otherwise permitted by applicable law;
- Comply with legal obligations, protect users and the service, and enforce applicable terms.

Payment information is not used to automatically enroll buyers in a recurring subscription. No payment-to-membership integration is claimed to exist before it is verified.

### 4. Providers and services

We use other services to operate Groundwork. Depending on the service, those providers currently may include **GitHub Pages** (public hosting), **Cloudflare** (domain, application delivery, Workers and the isolated payment database), **Supabase** (application database and authentication), **Resend** (where used for email delivery), **Google Analytics** (public-site analytics), and **Google Apps Script or related Google services** (beta request processing). Their handling of information is subject to the applicable service terms and privacy notices.

**Stripe** would be used for hosted checkout, payment processing and, where configured and applicable, tax calculation, refunds and payment-dispute information. Stripe may receive payment/billing details directly from the purchaser. We share or make available information to providers as needed for their contracted functions; providing a policy does not mean an unfinished integration is in operation.

### 5. Analytics, cookies, and similar technologies

The public site currently uses Google Analytics to understand general visitor activity. Analytics services may use cookies, IP addresses, browser/device information and similar identifiers. You can use supported browser or device controls to limit cookies. We do not use this statement to authorize the sale of your personal information or create new advertising data uses; practices must be reassessed if analytics or advertising tools change.

### 6. Financial and planning data

Groundwork is an information and planning tool, not a bank or financial institution. You control the financial and life-planning information you choose to provide. Avoid placing passwords, full card numbers, government ID numbers or other secrets in ordinary notes or fields. Bank account connections and future marketplace features must not be assumed available unless separately announced, implemented and covered by updated notices.

### 7. Sharing and disclosure

Able Systems does not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers for the purposes described here, as required by law or valid legal process, to investigate security incidents, prevent misuse, enforce rights or protect safety, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring or sale of the business. Where applicable law gives you additional rights concerning data sharing, those rights apply.

### 8. Retention and deletion

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for providing the applicable service, security, accounting, tax, contractual records, troubleshooting, refund/dispute handling and legal requirements. Retention depends on the type of record and the applicable obligation; **there is no single promised deletion period for all data**.

Where account deletion or privacy rights apply, we will evaluate your request and remove or limit information as required by law. Certain transaction, refund, security, legal-acceptance or backup records may need to remain for legitimate or legally required purposes, including after an account is closed. Records kept by providers such as Stripe may follow separate provider retention requirements.

### 9. Security and incidents

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet service or database can guarantee absolute security. Protect your login details and avoid sending sensitive card or identity information through ordinary support email.

If an incident requires notice under applicable law, we will investigate and provide notice to individuals, regulators or other parties as required.

### 10. Your choices and requests

You may decline optional development or promotional messages and use any unsubscribe methods provided. Necessary security, purchase, refund and account notices are different from marketing messages.

Depending on where you live and what law applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction, deletion or a copy of information we maintain about you, or to exercise other applicable privacy choices. Contact **hello@groundworkbyable.com** or **support@groundworkbyable.com** for privacy or account questions. For a purchase, refund or cancellation inquiry, contact **billing@groundworkbyable.com**. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting and may retain information where legally permitted or required.

Please do **not** email full card numbers, card verification codes, passwords or government ID numbers. Use a Stripe purchase reference and the contact address associated with a purchase when asking about a transaction.

### 11. Age and geographic eligibility

The invitation-only private beta is presently intended for adults **18 or older residing in the United States**, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly maintain private-beta accounts for anyone under 18. Any one-time individual pre-purchase will require its own appropriate age and geographic eligibility controls before launch. If you believe a minor has supplied personal information, contact us for review.

### 12. Changes to this policy

Our practices may evolve as features or services are introduced. We will publish the then-current Privacy Policy and its effective date on our public website and provide additional notice where the change is material or law requires it. A private review draft is not an effective policy.
