# Groundwork pre-purchase — refund, delay, and reserve policy for founder review

**STATUS: FOUNDER APPROVED POST-ACTIVATION REFUND STRUCTURE — November 14, 2026. OTHER TERMS STILL DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED OR LEGALLY CLEARED.**
**Scope:** Individual $9.99 one-time monthly pre-purchase and $96 one-time annual pre-purchase. Public landing site is targeted ASAP. Groundwork paid application target is January 1, 2027. No payment activation or application modification is authorized by this draft.

## Rules established by the founder (not new commercial offers)

- The marketing landing page is separate from the actual application and can launch sooner; official product launch target: **January 1, 2027**.
- Prepay cohort window **November 14–December 13, 2026** is a plan, not an active checkout. December 14–31 founders' early access, if technically ready, is **complimentary**.
- Monthly prepay $9.99 covers **January 2027**; **February 2027 free**; first possible separately authorized monthly renewal March 1, 2027.
- Annual prepay $96 covers **January 2027, plus March 2027–January 2028** (12 purchased months) with **February 2027 free**; service covered through January 31, 2028; next possible independently authorized annual renewal February 1, 2028.
- New eligible January customers get **one full complimentary first month** from their own actual usable activation date. Do not equate every new customer's renewal date with February 1.
- **All customer receipts** (prepaid and later ongoing payments) are subject to the founder's no-discretionary-spending-until-applicable-refund-period-has-passed policy and ongoing reserves for possible refunds, disputes, legal obligations, fees. Stripe payout is not the same as spendable funds.
- One-time Stripe prepay payments do **not** create recurring subscriptions or authorize saved-card renewals.
- Founding Access free reservations, the organization contracts, and unreleased Advanced/Assets/Marketplace have separate terms.

## Proposed policy to review — numbers and timing NOT approved

### 1. Before the paid term starts

**Recommendation:** Any customer who has prepaid can cancel before their purchased paid term starts and receive a **full refund**, including any tax refundable under applicable law, even if they received free December preview access. No cancellation fee. Process refund to the original payment method when possible.

**Proposed internal service level:** After validating the order and request, initiate the refund within **5 business days**. Communicate the initiation date and refund ID; the bank/processor determines when the credit appears. Do not promise funds instantly.

**Customer's request channel:** a verified, monitored support email and a public support/contact route. Current website lists `hello@groundworkbyable.com`, but payment/refund support and response workflow are not yet proven functional.

### 2. If the January 1 app launch is at risk

**January 1, 2027 remains the founder's release target.** This is *not* a postponement proposal.

- Alert affected buyers **when a delay becomes likely**, including what remains unfinished, whether usable access exists, revised estimate (if credible), full pre-activation refund option, and the contact route.
- Proposed routine checkpoint: send a readiness/update email **December 18, 2026** (14 days before January 1). This is **not** permission to promise a launch that isn't verified.
- If the app is not usable January 1, send a same-day delay update and repeat factual updates at least **weekly** until access or refund resolution. Do not silently treat a buyer's lack of reply as affirmative acceptance of an indefinite extension.
- Preserve the full purchased term and one free February-equivalent bonus period; the bonus must **shift with delayed delivery** instead of disappearing because February passes while the product is unavailable.
- **Still requires owner/legal sign-off:** a finite latest date by which an undelivered order must be fulfilled or proactively refunded; treatment of buyers who do not respond to a proposed delay; any mandatory refund rules for sales into each supported region.

### Proposed finite non-delivery resolution deadline — AWAITING FOUNDER APPROVAL

**Recommendation: January 15, 2027 as the latest date for a pre-purchase to remain unresolved without the purchaser's express agreement to a later, specific delivery date.** This is a **refund/consent resolution date**, NOT a postponement of the official January 1, 2027 application launch. If legal review establishes a stricter rule, follow it.

**Proposed exact sequence:**

| Date / condition | Customer treatment |
|---|---|
| **December 18, 2026** | Send pre-purchasers a factual readiness message, reiterate January 1 product launch target and unconditional pre-activation refund option |
| **As soon as a delay becomes likely** | Tell affected purchasers what is delayed and offer an immediate full refund; no waiting for the scheduled notice |
| **January 1, 2027, if usable service cannot open** | Notify each affected pre-purchaser of failure to meet the targeted opening, give a free immediate refund option, and request **affirmative** consent if the buyer wants to wait until a realistic published revised date |
| **January 8, 2027** | Send another factual status/decision reminder if any prepaid order is still undelivered |
| **January 15, 2027** | If paid access was still not delivered and the buyer did **not affirmatively agree** to a specific later delivery date, automatically cancel the undelivered order and initiate the full refund **within 5 business days**. Silence is never evidence of consent to an indefinite extension |
| **Buyer expressly agrees to a later delivery date** | Record exact consent timestamp, policy version and specific revised date; retain full refund option before delivered paid access, and refund if the agreed date is missed absent a new express agreement |
| **Buyer requests refund at any time before delivery** | Start refund handling immediately and aim to initiate within 5 business days after verified request, regardless of all other dates |

The dates **January 8 and January 15 are not launch targets** and must not be advertised as the actual application opening. No customer should be charged again for undelivered pre-purchased access. A one-time prepayment does not provide permission to charge future subscription payments.

**Bonus-month preservation:** For a late launch, shift the **entire paid term plus the entire one-month complimentary period** to the verified date usable access actually starts. The final billing dates and annual entitlement end date must be recomputed **per customer**, not simply left at the original January/February/March calendar labels. Exact calendar-anniversary handling and any December preview benefit require separate engineering verification. *Illustrative and not yet approved*: if a prepaid monthly user's usable access starts January 10, their purchased month could run January 10–February 9, and their complimentary full month February 10–March 9, with no authorized renewal before March 10.

**Unresponsive buyers:** Never presume their silence means they agreed to postpone fulfillment indefinitely. If the proposed January 15 cutoff arrives without access or recorded affirmative extension, refund proactively. If a buyer later signs up, start a separate, clearly consented purchase.

**Regulatory scope:** The FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order **Merchandise** Rule's automatic 30-day fulfillment clock does **not** cover services; the recommendation above is a proposed *customer contract and internal business policy*, not an asserted statutory 14-day rule. South Carolina Unfair Trade Practices Act, §39-5-20, prohibits unfair/deceptive acts or practices, and other state, payment-network and consumer requirements may apply. Obtain appropriate legal review before advertising contractual promises.

**Evidence and operational blockers:** verified buyer contact channel, email-delivery records, refund request/processing route, consent capture and audit log, revenue reserve, Stripe refunds and D1 reconciliation, ability to stop billing, and a support contact. None of these operational workflows has been verified as deployed or approved merely because this document exists.

### 3. Once usable paid access begins

**FOUNDER-APPROVED POLICY STRUCTURE (not yet legal-reviewed or published):**

- Allow a **full voluntary refund during the first 7 calendar days after the purchased paid term actually starts**. A complimentary December founders' preview is **not** the paid-term start and does not consume the refund window; actual usable paid access must exist for that paid term to start.
- **After the seven-day window:** annual buyers who cancel receive a prorated refund for **entirely unused future purchased months**. Each of the twelve months purchased for $96 is valued at **$8 before applicable tax adjustment**. **February's complimentary bonus is not a paid month**, and an undelivered paid month must not be counted as consumed. Do not refund a partly used current annual paid month under this voluntary unused-full-month formula, except where other rights apply.
- **After that window for the $9.99 prepaid month:** no ordinary voluntary partial-month refund for used service, except where access is not delivered, service defects, applicable law or other approved rights require a remedy.
- Any refund or cancellation must **not** result in a later unauthorized subscription charge. Separately consented renewals must have independently operable cancellation controls.

**Administrative details still open:** exactly how partial calendar months, a delayed launch, customer access not claimed, tax adjustments, cancellation cutoffs, and refund initiation timing are handled operationally; legal review; refund support; and final customer wording. A seven-day policy does not erase statutory or card-dispute rights.

**Founder reserve rule:** obligations to repay unused annual months can remain throughout the annual term. Maintain sufficient liquid reserve against remaining refund exposure, legal rights and disputes; **do not release the entire annual prepayment for discretionary spending after seven days**.

### 4. Unclaimed access

**Proposed:** Send onboarding instructions only when usable app access is verified. Send follow-ups after **7, 30, and 60 days** if no claim is recorded. Do not count an unread email as paid access; determine a fair refund/unclaimed-order cutoff before publication. Founder and legal review needed before forfeiture or automatic term expiration.

### 5. Reserve and financial controls

Do not call the reserve an escrow, trust or customer-protected bank account unless one truly exists and is appropriately structured. Every transaction should have:
- buyer/order reference, sale and tax amounts, paid-status evidence, Stripe fee and actual payout;
- agreed terms version, cohort/offer, delivered-access date, paid months earned, free months, renewal consent;
- refund requests/refunds/disputes and a conservative remaining refundable and legal-risk amount;
- a distinct **available-to-spend** ledger amount calculated **only after** applicable refund windows and reserve requirements, not merely on Stripe settlement.

**Never treat a refunded/disputed purchase as retained paid demand.** Revenue recognition and tax timing need bookkeeping/tax review.

## Source and legal applicability note

- FTC [Mail/Internet/Telephone Order **Merchandise** Rule guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule) says that rule **does not cover services**. Do **not** present its 30-day merchandise delivery/refund timetable as automatically applying to Groundwork SaaS. - Stripe [dispute guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/how-disputes-work) explains that disputes can debit funds and fees; the company must retain sufficient liquidity.
- Confirm any SC retail-license, tax classification, nexus, refund and digital-service rules with the relevant regulators or a qualified lawyer/tax advisor. **Not legal advice.**
