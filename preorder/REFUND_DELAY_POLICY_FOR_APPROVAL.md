# Groundwork pre-purchase — refund, delay, and reserve policy for founder review

**STATUS: DRAFT ONLY — November 14, 2026. NOT APPROVED, NOT PUBLISHED, NOT A LEGAL DETERMINATION.**
**Scope:** Individual $9.99 one-time monthly pre-purchase and $96 one-time annual pre-purchase. Public landing site is targeted ASAP. Groundwork paid application target is January 1, 2027. No payment activation or application modification is authorized by this draft.

## Rules established by the founder (not new commercial offers)

- The marketing landing page is separate from the actual application and can launch sooner; official product launch target: **January 1, 2027**.
- Prepay cohort window **November 14–December 13, 2026** is a plan, not an active checkout. December 14–31 founders' early access, if technically ready, is **complimentary**.
- Monthly prepay $9.99 covers **January 2027**; **February 2027 free**; first possible separately authorized monthly renewal March 1, 2027.
- Annual prepay $96 covers **January 2027, plus March 2027–January 2028** (12 purchased months) with **February 2027 free**; service covered through January 31, 2028; next possible independently authorized annual renewal February 1, 2028.
- New eligible January customers get **one full complimentary first month** from their own actual usable activation date. Do not equate every new customer's renewal date with February 1.
- **All customer receipts** (prepaid and later ongoing payments) are subject to the founder's no-discretionary-spending-until-applicable-refund-period-has-passed policy and ongoing reserves for possible refunds, disputes, legal obligations, fees. Stripe payout is not the same as spendable funds.
- One-time Stripe prepay payments do **not** create recurring subscriptions or authorize saved-card renewals.
- Founding Access free reservations, the organization contracts, and unreleased Advanced/Assets/Marketplace have separate terms.

## Proposed policy to review — numbers and timing NOT approved

### 1. Before the paid term starts

**Recommendation:** Any customer who has prepaid can cancel before their purchased paid term starts and receive a **full refund**, including any tax refundable under applicable law, even if they received free December preview access. No cancellation fee. Process refund to the original payment method when possible.

**Proposed internal service level:** After validating the order and request, initiate the refund within **5 business days**. Communicate the initiation date and refund ID; the bank/processor determines when the credit appears. Do not promise funds instantly.

**Customer's request channel:** a verified, monitored support email and a public support/contact route. Current website lists `hello@groundworkbyable.com`, but payment/refund support and response workflow are not yet proven functional.

### 2. If the January 1 app launch is at risk

**January 1, 2027 remains the founder's release target.** This is *not* a postponement proposal.

- Alert affected buyers **when a delay becomes likely**, including what remains unfinished, whether usable access exists, revised estimate (if credible), full pre-activation refund option, and the contact route.
- Proposed routine checkpoint: send a readiness/update email **December 18, 2026** (14 days before January 1). This is **not** permission to promise a launch that isn't verified.
- If the app is not usable January 1, send a same-day delay update and repeat factual updates at least **weekly** until access or refund resolution. Do not silently treat a buyer's lack of reply as affirmative acceptance of an indefinite extension.
- Preserve the full purchased term and one free February-equivalent bonus period; the bonus must **shift with delayed delivery** instead of disappearing because February passes while the product is unavailable.
- **Still requires owner/legal sign-off:** a finite latest date by which an undelivered order must be fulfilled or proactively refunded; treatment of buyers who do not respond to a proposed delay; any mandatory refund rules for sales into each supported region.

### 3. Once usable paid access begins

**Proposed, not approved:**
- Allow a **full voluntary refund within 7 calendar days** after the *paid term* actually starts (not after the promotional December preview).
- After that voluntary period, annual buyers requesting cancellation receive a refund based on the base value of **entirely unused future purchased months**. For the $96 offer, each of the 12 purchased months represents **$8** before applicable tax adjustment; the free February month is not counted as a paid month. Do not count any undelivered month as used.
- No ordinary pro-rata refund for a partly used one-month prepaid term after the 7-day grace period, except where service nonperformance, legal protections, or other approved customer policy require it.
- Refund/termination should never result in a future unauthorized subscription charge. If the customer separately opts into renewal, the cancellation route must address that subscription independently.

**Operational cost of this generous annual policy:** the risk of returning unused annual months extends throughout the annual term. Keep an appropriately liquid reserve for unused service, statutory/refund rights and possible chargebacks; **do not release the entire annual purchase for spending merely after seven days**. Founder must choose whether they prefer this or a narrower, lawful cancellation policy.

### 4. Unclaimed access

**Proposed:** Send onboarding instructions only when usable app access is verified. Send follow-ups after **7, 30, and 60 days** if no claim is recorded. Do not count an unread email as paid access; determine a fair refund/unclaimed-order cutoff before publication. Founder and legal review needed before forfeiture or automatic term expiration.

### 5. Reserve and financial controls

Do not call the reserve an escrow, trust or customer-protected bank account unless one truly exists and is appropriately structured. Every transaction should have:
- buyer/order reference, sale and tax amounts, paid-status evidence, Stripe fee and actual payout;
- agreed terms version, cohort/offer, delivered-access date, paid months earned, free months, renewal consent;
- refund requests/refunds/disputes and a conservative remaining refundable and legal-risk amount;
- a distinct **available-to-spend** ledger amount calculated **only after** applicable refund windows and reserve requirements, not merely on Stripe settlement.

**Never treat a refunded/disputed purchase as retained paid demand.** Revenue recognition and tax timing need bookkeeping/tax review.

## Source and legal applicability note

- FTC [Mail/Internet/Telephone Order **Merchandise** Rule guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule) says that rule **does not cover services**. Do **not** present its 30-day merchandise delivery/refund timetable as automatically applying to Groundwork SaaS. General unfair/deceptive practice law, state consumer-protection law, payment-network requirements, and an enforceable customer contract may still apply.
- Stripe [refund guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds) explains that Stripe's original processing fees generally are not returned and insufficient funds can delay refunds.
- Stripe [dispute guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/disputes/how-disputes-work) explains that disputes can debit funds and fees; the company must retain sufficient liquidity.
- Confirm any SC retail-license, tax classification, nexus, refund and digital-service rules with the relevant regulators or a qualified lawyer/tax advisor. **Not legal advice.**
