A few honest answers

You don't have to have it figured out to begin.

Do I need to be good with money?

No. Groundwork is designed for people managing very different realities, including changing income, complicated obligations, and new beginnings.

What does testing Groundwork involve?

Exploring the features available to you and, if you'd like, describing what is useful, confusing, or missing. There is no need for technical expertise.

Does submitting a request guarantee an invitation?

No. Private-beta invitations are reviewed and opened in controlled waves. Completing the form does not create an account.

Is the private beta paid?

Groundwork does not plan to charge invited testers during the beta testing period. Any later paid offerings would be communicated separately.

Are Assets & Inventory or bank connections available?

Assets & Inventory is being developed and is not currently open to general beta participants. Bank connections are planned for later.

A note from the founder

Groundwork began with a question.

How much of my working life does it take to support the life I'm living?

That question became a foundation for understanding money, time, and responsibility together. Groundwork is growing, and real people's experiences matter as we refine it.

You don't have to have everything figured out to begin building a foundation.
