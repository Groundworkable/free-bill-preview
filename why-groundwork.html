The question that started it

How much working life does my life require?

It's not about being told what to buy, what to save, or what choices you're allowed to make. It's about seeing the full cost of the life you're already supporting.

Groundwork translates appropriate dollar amounts into hours and minutes, keeps recurring commitments visible, and distinguishes money you have now from money you're still expecting.

The purpose isn't guilt. It's understanding.
