What a sponsored place includes — and what happens next

One sponsored place represents proposed personal Groundwork access for one participant during an agreed term, limited to capabilities verified at activation. It does not give the sponsor access to that person's private financial records.

Provisioning, seat reassignment, support levels, integrations, and custom services or reporting require separate scoping. After a 90-day pilot, continuation is a separate decision; post-sponsorship access terms remain to be agreed.

For schools: start with a scoped discovery conversation. Classroom and student-data functionality would require separate development and privacy review; no unapproved school price is shown.
